# Paper 1 YOLO12n SVRDD7 handoff — 2026-09-09

## Scope

This branch starts the YOLO12n-based Paper 1 route on SVRDD7. YOLO12 keeps its native Detect head, TAL assignment, BCE classification, CIoU/DFL regression, and NMS evaluation. Test remains sealed and current comparisons use Val, `imgsz=640`, `batch=32`, `seed=42`, and no Voting.

## TAL/DFL diagnosis

The frozen 100E B0 diagnosis found 2,611 of 2,612 GTs with TAL positives and 23,818 positive candidates. Native same-GT score ordering was weak, while the TAL target itself ordered candidates much more consistently. Small-object positive IoU was substantially below medium and large objects. These observations motivated a training-only same-GT ranking probe; they did not establish that explicit pairwise ranking was the correct repair.

## Rejected experiment: same-GT residual ranking

The 30E candidate adds no parameters and changes no inference behavior. It applies a training-only RankNet-style loss to TAL-positive candidates assigned to the same GT, using detached current-box IoU differences as pair targets.

| Metric | B0 30E | Ranking 30E | Delta (pp) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| AP50:95 | 38.338 | 37.891 | -0.447 |
| AP50 | 63.428 | 63.382 | -0.046 |
| AP75 | 40.456 | 39.918 | -0.538 |
| APsmall | 14.800 | 13.918 | -0.881 |
| APmedium | 32.833 | 33.174 | +0.341 |
| APlarge | 53.645 | 58.694 | +5.049 |
| AR100 | 59.864 | 59.737 | -0.127 |

Matched mechanism checks also failed: weighted same-GT pair accuracy fell from 0.4293 to 0.4180, score-IoU Spearman fell from -0.0473 to -0.0580, top-score positive IoU was unchanged, and best-positive IoU decreased. Training time increased by about 21.7%. The isolated APlarge increase does not override the overall, AP75, APsmall, and mechanism regressions.

Decision: **NO-GO**. Do not rescue-scan ranking weight, margin, temperature, or pair count, and do not combine this loss with later modules.

## Next causal route

The training-free YOLO12n P2-to-P3 early-detail survival diagnosis has now been completed on the frozen official 100E B0 checkpoint. It used SVRDD7 Val 1000, original-image COCO scale groups, continuous target/ring sampling, five-fold image-grouped linear probes, and 5,000 image-paired bootstrap repetitions. No visualization was generated.

For small non-hit GTs, P2 and P3 probe AUC were 0.8022 and 0.7951. Target-background contrast increased from 0.05049 at P2 to 0.08806 at P3; the paired P2-to-P3 change was +0.03757 with 95% CI [+0.01342, +0.06198]. Small hit/non-hit separation was larger at P3 than P2. Elongated targets also gained contrast from P2 to P3, and every class had a positive P2-to-P3 contrast change with a positive 95% CI.

Decision: **NO-GO for P2-to-P3 detail injection.** The predeclared early-detail-collapse pattern was absent. The single next causal route is a training-free P3 regression, DFL-distribution, and scale-conditioned localization audit. Do not add P2 injection, retry same-GT ranking, or train a new model before that audit.
